Motion test

This page checks what your phone's browser reports. Tell Muse what the lines below say.

Reduced motion reported: checking…
WebGL available: checking…
Animation frames running: 0

The orange bar above should slide left and right, and the frame counter should keep counting up.

Screen: …
Browser: …

If the bar does not move and the counter stays at 0, your phone or browser is blocking animation itself. If the bar does move but the EMBER & PALM site does not, your phone is reporting "reduced motion: YES" — the site is obeying that setting. The fix is then: Settings → Accessibility → Remove animations → off (Samsung: Accessibility → Visibility enhancements → Reduce animations), then reload the site.
